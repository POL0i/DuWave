**DuWave is a dynamic local and open-source music player, designed to offer the best experience both offline and online.**

**Main features:**
* **Online Search & Shazam Integration:** Find and listen to music directly from the internet, and identify songs playing around you using our custom Shazam Node server.
* **Music Downloading:** Save your favorite songs for offline listening.
* **Smooth Playback:** Optimized audio support powered by ExoPlayer.
* **Dynamic Interface:** Utilizing the Palette API, the UI automatically changes and adapts to the colors of your album covers.
* **Audio Visualizers:** Real-time, highly-customizable audio visualizers (including Pixel Art styles, Procedural CRT, and more).
* **Built-in Audio Trimmer:** Cut and trim your music files effortlessly.
* **Library Management:** Comprehensive local music library and playlist handling.
* **Advanced Equalizer:** Multiple profiles to perfectly tune your audio.

**🌟 What's new in v2.1.1:**
* **UI Polish:** Fixed critical text legibility and contrast issues across the app (especially in the language selection and dropdown menus) when using dark backgrounds.
* **New Free Visual Styles:** Procedural CRT and Clouds (Terraria Water) are now completely free for everyone!
* **More Effects:** Added new 'SineWave' and 'Polygon Unfold' visual effects.
