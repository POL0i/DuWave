DuWave is a dynamic local and open-source music player, designed to offer the best experience both offline and online.

Main features:
* Online search and streaming: Find and listen to music directly from the internet.
* Music downloading: Save your favorite songs for offline listening.
* Smooth playback and optimized support via ExoPlayer.
* Dynamic interface with Palette API that changes and adapts to the album covers of your songs.
* Real-time audio visualizer.
* Comprehensive local music library and playlist management.
* Built-in audio trimmer.
* Advanced equalizer with multiple profiles and unique visual styles (including Pixel Art style).
* Advanced customization options for changing album covers based on titles.
