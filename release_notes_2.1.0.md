# Release v2.1.0

We are excited to announce **DuWave v2.1.0**, packed with huge new features, remodeled components, and brand new visual styles! 

## 🌟 What's New

* **🎶 Song Identification (Shazam integration):** Ever wondered what song is playing in the background? DuWave now features a brand new Shazam integration! We added a new *Live Microphone* subsection that securely captures ambient audio without recording anything to storage.
* **✂️ Remodeled Audio Trimmer:** The audio cutting and trimming section has been completely redesigned from the ground up. It’s now easier and more precise to cut your local audio files and create custom ringtones.
* **🎨 New Visual Styles & Backgrounds:** We have added stunning new real-time wave styles and background variations. 
  * Included new **Free** styles available for all users.
  * Added exclusive, premium **Patreon-only** visual styles and backgrounds.
* **🖼️ New Frames:** Personalize your player cover with new free frames, as well as exclusive frames for our Patreon supporters.

## 🐛 Bug Fixes & Improvements

* Fixed various crashes occurring when changing application language or switching to specific visual styles (like the Hearts style).
* Greatly improved the interaction for the vinyl gesture on the player cover, removing stuttering and ensuring the cover art returns smoothly to its original position.
* Adjusted the playlist gesture hitbox to make it more reliable and prevent it from conflicting with the vinyl rotation gesture.
* Boosted FPS optimization for background styles and visualizations across the app.
* Resolved a bug with ProGuard/R8 shrinking that caused streaming and online search functionalities to disappear in release builds.
